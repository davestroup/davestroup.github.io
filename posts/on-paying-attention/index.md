There are things that don’t need to become an essay. A detail from a walk. A sentence worth keeping. A thought that needs somewhere to land.

A journal can accommodate unfinished thoughts. It can hold a small observation without asking it to justify an entire article.

## A little room

The shape of an entry can follow the thing itself: sometimes a few paragraphs, sometimes a photograph, sometimes both.
